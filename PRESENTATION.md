## Business value (speak these, don’t over-claim)

1. **Time.** Prep drops from the best part of an hour to a review. Six SDRs, six hours a week, is a full-time hire returned to selling.
2. **Aftermarket.** Every DRO older than ~24 months becomes a membrane/service play instead of silence. One extra spares job per engineer per month pays for the pilot.
3. **Quote integrity.** We stop recycling 2019 prices. Sizing runs on a **new lab**, so FATs fail less often.
4. **Credit.** Open invoices sit on the same 360 as the chase. New BOQs don’t fly while AR is on fire.
5. **Meetings.** Evidence-backed notes beat spray-and-pray. Track reply rate and labs received, not emails sent.

## Pilot ask (leave this on the slide)

- One country, one salesperson code, **draft-only for 4 weeks**.
- Metrics: draft acceptance %, alerts acted on, lab reports into sizing, quotes on agent-prepped accounts.
- Kill criteria: factual error rate or customer complaints.

## Do not say

- That we auto-send email or LinkedIn.
- That we replace the sales engineer.
- Exact revenue guarantees.
- That Streamlit Cloud SQLite is the system of record (BC is).
